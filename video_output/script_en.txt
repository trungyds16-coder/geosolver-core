Welcome educators and students.
Today, we are pleased to introduce GeoSolver 2D CAD, an educational application designed to support plane geometry learning and teaching.

The software provides an intuitive Cartesian workspace on the Oxy coordinate plane.
Constructing points, line segments, and triangles is straightforward, seamlessly connecting synthetic geometry with coordinate geometry.

A notable feature of GeoSolver is the automated geometric invariant detector.
As geometric figures are drawn, the system recognizes properties such as isosceles triangles, right angles, and displays exact lengths using simplified radicals.

Teachers and students can readily construct triangle incircles and circumcircles with automatic center calculation.
Distinct color coding enhances visual clarity for classroom demonstrations.

In addition to standard curriculum topics, GeoSolver includes advanced geometry presets, such as verifying the Euler circle passing through a fixed point, complemented by comprehensive algebraic object tables.

GeoSolver 2D CAD is developed to accompany classrooms in every geometry lesson.
